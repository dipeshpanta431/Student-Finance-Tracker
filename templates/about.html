PROJECT OVERVIEW

About Student Finance Tracker

Student Finance Tracker is a web-based personal financial management application developed to help students efficiently record, organize, and monitor their financial activities. The application provides an easy-to-use platform where users can manage daily income and expenses, create monthly budgets, and analyze financial data through interactive charts and comprehensive reports.

The system emphasizes simplicity, accessibility, and financial awareness by enabling users to understand their spending behavior, monitor budget utilization, and generate downloadable financial reports. Through its clean interface and analytical tools, the application assists students in making informed financial decisions and developing better money management habits.
