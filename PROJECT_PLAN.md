# Smart Student Expense Management System

## Problem Statement

Students often struggle to manage their income, expenses, and monthly budgets. Most rely on manual methods or do not track their spending at all, making it difficult to identify spending patterns and achieve savings goals.

## Objective

To develop a web-based expense management system using Python and Flask that helps students manage their finances through expense tracking, budgeting, savings goals, reports, and AI-powered financial insights.

## Main Modules

- User Management
- Income Management
- Expense Management
- Budget Management
- Savings Goals
- Dashboard & Reports
- AI Financial Insights
